## Direction 1 — Signal

Inspired by:

- oscilloscopes
- radio transmissions
- spectrograms
- waveform scans
- CRT monitors
- analog interference

Very monochromatic.

Black.
White.
Occasional fluorescent green or amber.
Think:
early industrial design
Swiss typography
broadcast graphics

## Direction 2 — Noise

Use visual interference.

Misregistration.

Static.

Broken grids.

Offset printing artifacts.

Everything feels almost, but not quite aligned.

The "signal" becomes the clean element among chaos.

Physical imperfections.
