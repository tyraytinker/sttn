The room holds what the night makes.

Every STTN event runs a local network. You join it from your phone. Photographs from the floor, work the artists leave — it stays on [[The Box|the box]]. It does not go online. It is never trained on. It is never advertised against.

Tonight is what you can see. Each night is its own folder. STTN keeps the disk. A past night opens only if someone in the room opens it on purpose.

You can look.
You can listen, if an artist left sound.
You can print before you leave.

You do not take the file. If you want it again, you come back.

---

## The contract

[[Presence]] says there is no livestream and no endless archive. Space is how that becomes true in the hand, not only in the policy.

The network is not a platform. It is part of the room — like the PA, the lights, the door. [[Human|People over platforms]]. Not anti-technology. A network that refuses to behave like one.

STTN holds the nights. The public does not. What phones see is this gathering, unless a past night is opened in the room.

## What lives here

Two kinds of leaving. Same rule: the file stays.

**Floor.** Anyone on the network can put a photograph on the wall of tonight. You can look. You can print.

**Artists.** A night code unlocks a drop. Stills and flyers to look at. Sound to listen to in the room. Stills can be printed. The set does not go home.

Nothing is ranked. Nothing is recommended. Discovery still happens by [[Discovery|ears]], and by walking up to a page or a print.

## Print

The sanctioned way out is paper.

You do not take the file. You take an object.

That object can return. A print can be scanned back in on another night. The archive grows by hands, not by feeds.

## What this is not

Not a recap email.
Not a highlight reel.
Not a cloud with a password.
Not a folder you save to the phone.
Not a story that expires so it can be sold as scarcity.

The scarcity is real: you had to be in the room, and you have to come back.
