Night 01

[Projections]

Signal Records · Wicker Park · 1343 N. Ashland Ave. · Sept 17 · 6–9p

Featuring

Alex Delgado

alx.delgado is keeping Chicago’s house roots alive while pushing the sound forward. Blending early 2000s house sounds with a psychedelic, progressive, and electro flair throughout the decades. Inspired by time spent in London digging through shops and soaking up the abundance of club nights into the early mornings, patiently observing and building a collection of gems for the audience.

“I grew a large inspiration from early house and freestyle records my dad gave me when he was partying in Chicago in the late 80’s. While living abroad I explored the depths of the London underground house and minimal scene, frequenting clubs such as Fabric, 93 Feet East, Star Lane. I dug for records a lot in the UK and have always aimed to take those experiences and recreate them for the dance floor.”
