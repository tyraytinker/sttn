You have to be there.

A private showcase that only opens for people who signed the commitment and walked into the room.

Night rundown

  1. Commitment at the door.

    Entry begins with the Commitment to Human Music. Everything played and shown that night is made entirely by people.

  2. Box on. WAN dark.

    The lamp is lit. The room network stays local so the night remains in the room.

  3. Tonight starts.

    One night, one folder. That is what phones on the network can see.

  4. The door is a card.

    SSID and a short address on paper. Join the room network from your phone.

  5. Phones join the room.

    Upload, look, print. Listen when artists leave sound for the night.

  6. Paper at the station.

    You leave with a print from the station. The file stays on the box.

  7. Pack-out.

    Power off. The disk stays with STTN until another room opens it on purpose.
