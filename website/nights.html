Night rundown

  1. Box on. WAN dark.

    The lamp is lit. The radio is up. There is no guest network to the public internet.

  2. Tonight starts.

    Each event is its own folder. Tonight is the only night the public site mounts.

  3. The door is a card.

    SSID and a short address, printed. The phone's "Sign in to network" sheet is the site. Artists get a second code for the drop.

  4. Phones join the room.

    Floor: upload, look, print. Artists: listen, look, print stills. No ranking, likes, or comments.

  5. Paper at the station.

    The job is a button on the phone. The object lands at the printer. Someone has to unjam it. That is a role.

  6. Pack-out.

    Power off. The disk stays with STTN. Next event, new folder. Old nights exist, and stay closed until someone in a room opens one.
