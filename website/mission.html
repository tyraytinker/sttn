Mission / Manifesto

Human music is worth protecting.

Simply believing human expression has intrinsic value.

STTN is not a platform and not a protest. It is a series of rooms where the night is the point.

Not nostalgic. Not anti-technology. We use machines. We refuse to let them behave like feeds.

People over platforms.

Artists deserve to be discovered by ears, not algorithms. Discovery still happens the slow way: walking into a room, standing still, listening.

Presence

You have to be there. No livestream. No endless archive. The event exists because people gathered in one room.

The commitment

The Commitment to Human Music turns attendance into participation. People aren't just buying a ticket. They're joining something.

That something is small on purpose. Almost secretive. A network that is part of the room, like the PA, the lights, the door.
