Mission / STTN · Chicago

Ensure the artist is heard.

A private art and sound showcase built around human music.

Signal Through the Noise brings business owners, musicians, curators, DJs, and locals into the same room. Together we elevate the sound of human music—every track played and every work shown is made entirely by people.

Physical media is resistance.

Human musicians carry culture in a language that still speaks to a lived life. When we raise their sound, we sharpen how a neighborhood hears itself. Records, rooms, and nights take time; we lean in to the friction.

Commitment to Human Music

The commitment asks you, as a discerning listener, to keep choosing human music. Entry to an STTN night begins with your signature.
