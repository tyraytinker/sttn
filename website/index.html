01 / Human

Commitment

02 / The box

The room holds what the night makes.

Every STTN event runs a local network. You join it from your phone. Photographs from the floor and work the artists leave stay on the box. Nothing goes online. Nothing gets trained on or advertised against.

Look. Listen, if an artist left sound. Print before you leave. You do not take the file. If you want it again, you come back.
