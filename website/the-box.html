Venue gear / The box

The box travels with STTN.

In the room it becomes the network. One machine. No uplink.

It is part of the room.

Phones join it and get a website that only exists here. A printer is attached to the same machine.

The network is not a platform. It is venue gear: PA, lights, door, this.

WAN unplugged. Nothing on this machine routes to the internet. A visible status: closed. If the lamp is dark, the night is lying.

STTN holds the nights. The public does not. What phones see is this gathering, unless a past night is opened in the room — on purpose.

Floor

Tonight’s wall.

Anyone on the network can put a photograph on the wall of tonight. You can look. You can print. No download. No files folder. Nothing is ranked. Nothing is recommended.

Artists

A night code. A drop.

One night code, not accounts. Unlocks stills and flyers to look at, sound to listen to in the room. Stills can be printed. The set does not go home.

Print

The sanctioned way out is paper.

You do not take the file. You take an object. That object can return. A print can be scanned back in on another night. The archive grows by hands, not by feeds.

What this is not

  • 01 Not a recap email.
  • 02 Not a highlight reel.
  • 03 Not a cloud with a password.
  • 04 Not a folder you save to the phone.
  • 05 Not a story that expires so it can be sold as scarcity.

The scarcity is real: you had to be in the room, and you have to come back. The box guarantees STTN does not leak, train, or advertise. It does not police a camera. We say that at the door.
